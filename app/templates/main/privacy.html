Privacy notice

You don't need an account to use Citizen Compass, and we collect as little information as we can.

What we never ask for

We never ask for your National ID number, passwords or payment details. Please don't enter them anywhere on this site.

Your checklist

Items you tick on a checklist stay in your own browser. They aren't sent to us.

Searches and page views

We keep the words people search for, with long numbers removed, and daily totals of page views. We use these to learn which information citizens need. They aren't linked to you.

Reports you send us

If you report a problem, we keep your report so we can correct our information. Contact details are optional. If you give them, we use them only to follow up on that report.

Technical logs

Like most websites, the server may keep short technical logs to keep the site working and secure.

Data protection law

This project aims to follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. This notice will be reviewed before any public launch.
